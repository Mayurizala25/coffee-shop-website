Our story

We started with one drum roaster and a very patient landlord.

Ten years ago this was a two-table room with a 5 kg roaster in the back and a queue that went out to the street by nine. We kept the queue and the roaster. The tables multiplied.

We buy from eleven farms, four of them within a day's drive. Every lot is roasted in batches under 12 kg, cupped the next morning, and pulled from the shelf after fourteen days. What doesn't sell gets brewed for the staff, not blended away.
